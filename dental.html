Common questions

Does teeth whitening damage my enamel?
No. Our professional whitening uses enamel-safe gels applied and monitored by a dentist, with a desensitizer afterwards. It's very different from over-the-counter kits — most patients feel no sensitivity at all.
Do you accept my insurance?
We work with all major dental insurance providers and will check your coverage before treatment, so you know your exact out-of-pocket cost upfront. For treatments over $300 we also offer 0% monthly payment plans.
What should I do in a dental emergency?
Call us on (555) 248-0137. If you call before noon, we hold same-day emergency slots every day we're open. For a knocked-out tooth, hold it by the crown (not the root), keep it in milk, and get to us within the hour if you can.
How long do dental implants last?
With normal care, implants can last decades — often a lifetime. Every implant at BrightSmile carries a 10-year warranty, and we check it free at your regular check-ups.
I'm nervous about visiting the dentist. Can you help?
Absolutely — nervous patients are most of our new patients. We use painless injection techniques, explain each step before it happens, and offer sedation options for longer treatments. You can stop us at any time with a raised hand.
